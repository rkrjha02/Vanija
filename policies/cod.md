# Cash on Delivery Policy

> MADE-UP EXAMPLE for testing this project. Edit before any real use.

## How Cash on Delivery works
You pay the courier in cash when the order arrives. Cash on Delivery is currently the only payment method.

## Paying at the door
Please keep the exact amount ready, because the courier may not carry change. Card and UPI payments at the door are not accepted.

## Checking the package
You can check the outer package for damage before paying. You cannot open the product box before paying.

## Laptops
For laptop orders, we send a one-time code (OTP) to the phone number on the order. Give the code to the courier after paying.

## Refusing a Cash on Delivery order
You may refuse an order at the door. If you refuse orders many times, we may ask you to confirm future orders by phone before shipping.
