# Returns Policy

> MADE-UP EXAMPLE for testing this project. Edit before any real use.

## How to request a return
Ask in the chat or contact customer support with your order ID. We arrange a pickup from your delivery address. Items must be returned with all accessories, manuals and the original box.

## Phones
Phones can be returned within 7 days of delivery only if they are defective or not as described. A used phone with no defect cannot be returned. Before pickup, reset the phone and remove all accounts (for example your Google account or Apple ID).

## Laptops
Laptops can be returned within 10 days of delivery only if they are defective or not as described. If the seal on the box is broken, the return is accepted only after our technician confirms the defect at pickup.

## Headphones
Unopened headphones can be returned within 7 days of delivery for any reason. Opened headphones and earphones cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they are defective. Defective opened headphones are replaced, not refunded.

## Refunds
After the returned item passes inspection, the refund is processed within 5 business days. For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund is sent by bank transfer to an account you provide.

## Items that cannot be returned
Products with physical damage, liquid damage, or missing parts caused after delivery cannot be returned. Free gifts that came with a product must be returned together with it.
