# Warranty Policy

> MADE-UP EXAMPLE for testing this project. Edit before any real use.

## Phones
Phones have a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects. The battery is covered for 6 months. Water or liquid damage, physical damage such as a cracked screen, and damage from repairs outside an authorized service center are not covered.

## Laptops
Laptops have a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. The battery and charger are covered for 6 months. Liquid damage, physical damage, and software problems such as viruses are not covered.

## Headphones
Headphones and earphones have a 6-month warranty from the delivery date. It covers defects such as one side not working or a charging fault. Lost earbuds, normal wear of ear tips and cables, and water damage are not covered.

## How to make a warranty claim
Take the product and its invoice to the brand's authorized service center. You can ask for a copy of your invoice in the chat. The store itself does not repair products.

## Replaced products
A product replaced under warranty keeps the remaining warranty of the original product. The warranty does not restart.
