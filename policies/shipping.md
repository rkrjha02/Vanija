# Shipping Policy

> MADE-UP EXAMPLE for testing this project. Edit before any real use.

## Delivery areas
We deliver across India. Some remote pincodes take longer than usual.

## Delivery time
Orders reach metro cities in 2 to 4 business days and other cities in 4 to 7 business days. Remote areas can take up to 10 business days.

## Shipping charges
Delivery is free on all orders.

## Laptops
Laptops are shipped in tamper-proof packaging. Check that the seal is unbroken before accepting the package. If the seal is broken, refuse the delivery and tell us in the chat.

## Delivery updates
We send delivery updates by SMS to the phone number on the order.

## Failed delivery
The courier tries to deliver 3 times. If all attempts fail, the order comes back to us and is cancelled.
